## 2. Stock Doubling Strategy (Raw Butcher Cut ⇄ Cooked Presentation)
Per your requirement:
> *"All similar images should double and act as the product stock. not duplicate images. similar images of the same product."*

Every product features two distinct, synchronized photography perspectives:
- **Angle 1 (Raw Butcher Specification):** Shows the raw cut on butcher paper or prep boards, displaying fat cap trim, intermuscular marbling, bone structure, and raw weight portioning.
- **Angle 2 (Cooked / Plated Presentation):** Shows the reverse-seared, smoked, flame-grilled, or carved dish, triggering sensory appetite appeal and recipe inspiration.

This doubles customer engagement while giving your catalog a high-end editorial feel without repetitive duplicate photos.
