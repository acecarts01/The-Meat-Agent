## 6. Australian Market Inelasticity & SEO Ranking Strategy

### Which categories are most inelastic (highest customer loyalty & recession-proof)?

1. **Everyday Family Staples (Maximum Inelasticity):**
   - *Products:* 1kg/2kg Beef Mince, Thin BBQ Sausages, Angus Burger Patties, Casserole Diced Beef.
   - *Why:* Australian families purchase these weekly regardless of economic swings. High order frequency drives lifetime customer value (LTV).

2. **Low & Slow American BBQ (Passionate Pitmasters - Zero Price Sensitivity):**
   - *Products:* Whole Packers Brisket (Point & Flat), Asado Short Ribs, Pork Ribs, Beef Cheeks.
   - *Why:* Smoker enthusiasts in Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane spend $150–$300 per cook without hesitation. Supermarkets cannot cut true competition-spec brisket or beef cheeks, giving you an uncontested niche.

3. **High-Protein & Gym Meal-Prep Bundles (Subscription-Like Habit):**
   - *Products:* 10-Pack Lean Rumps, 2kg Chicken Breast & Steak combos.
   - *Why:* Health and fitness professionals and gym-goers treat high-protein meat as an essential recurring expense.

4. **Wagyu & Dry-Aged Luxury Steaks (High Average Order Value):**
   - *Products:* 900g Tomahawks, Bistecca alla Fiorentina, Scotch Fillets.
   - *Why:* Replaces expensive restaurant dinners (where a Wagyu steak costs $120+). Customers gladly spend $60–$90 online for restaurant-grade meat delivered home.
